Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short: TopperKit is built for school students, so we collect as little as we can, never sell personal data, never show targeted ads to children, and ask a parent or guardian for consent before a student under 18 uses an account.

This policy explains how TopperKit Edutech Private Limited ("TopperKit", "we", "us") handles personal data when you use our website topperkit.com and the TopperKit app (together, the "Service"). We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 with its rules, as applicable in India.

1. Who is responsible

TopperKit Edutech Private Limited, Kadayanallur - 627751, Tamil Nadu, India, is the Data Fiduciary for personal data processed through the Service. Contact: support@topperkit.com.

We do not ask for a student's photograph, exact location, contacts, school name or government ID numbers.

3. Children and parental consent

  • Anyone under 18 is a child under Indian law. We obtain verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before creating or activating an account for a child.
  • We do not track or monitor the behaviour of children for advertising, and we do not direct targeted advertising at children.
  • We will not process a child's data in any way likely to cause them harm.
  • A parent or guardian can review, correct or delete the child's data, or withdraw consent, at any time by writing to support@topperkit.com.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and personalise the Service (class-wise books, exercises and study resources).
  • Keep the Service safe, prevent misuse and fix errors.
  • Understand, in aggregate, which features help students most.
  • Respond to queries and send essential service or account messages.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party marketing.

5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who process it for us under written obligations (for example cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting and messaging providers), and with authorities when required by law. Providers may be located outside India; where so, we require appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as the account is active or as needed for the purposes above, and then delete or anonymise it, unless law requires us to retain it longer. Inactive accounts may be deleted after a reasonable period.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring. No system is completely secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You (or a parent or guardian for a child) may: access a summary of data we hold; correct or update it; request erasure; withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise rights if you are unable to; and raise a grievance. Write to support@topperkit.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and, in any case, within the timelines the law prescribes.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses only essential technologies needed to load and secure pages. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

10. Third-party links

The Service may link to external sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or on this page, and where consent is needed we will ask again.
